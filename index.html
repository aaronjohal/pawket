What We Stock

Curated essentials for dogs on the go.

Every Pawket machine is stocked with a carefully curated range of premium pet products — sourced from quality distributors and trusted brands.

Treats & Chews

Natural treats, training rewards, dental chews, and long-lasting chews from premium brands.

Accessories

Leads, collapsible bowls, travel pouches, bandanas, and on-the-go essentials.

Hydration

Portable water bottles, pet-safe hydration sachets, and electrolyte supplements.

Hygiene

Poo bags, biodegradable rolls, wet wipes, paw cleaning mitts, and sanitising sprays.

Toys & Play

Compact toys, tennis balls, tug ropes, and enrichment items for active dogs.
